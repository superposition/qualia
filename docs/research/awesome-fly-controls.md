# Awesome Fly: controls, interactive worlds, language and art audit

Reviewed 2026-09-12. Scope: all links in the **Games and control experiments**, **Desktop flies and interactive worlds**, and **Language, art, and other experiments** sections of [cobanov/awesome-fly](https://github.com/cobanov/awesome-fly). This is a source audit, not an independent reproduction. No projects were installed or executed, no GPU jobs ran, and no robot or trading interfaces were called.

Coverage is **36 project repositories, six explicitly listed demo pages, and the explicitly listed Fly Dino methods page: 43/43 top-level links attempted**. All 36 project READMEs and the methods page were retrieved. All six demo URLs returned HTTP 200 through a direct public HTTP read after the browsing tool rejected them; four exposed only JavaScript shells. No interactive demo was exercised. Additional methods, result reports, licenses and deployment pages were inspected below. Current branch documents can change; these conclusions describe the material retrieved on this date.

The most useful result is methodological: several projects that visibly move, produce spikes or change weights explicitly fail visual-control, learning, or topology-benefit tests. None of the reviewed projects establishes a ready-made transfer from our robot camera through the released MaleCNS model to successful physical exploration. They do provide concrete architectures and tests for building that capability.

## What to implement in Qualia

This is an engineering proposal inferred from the audited sources, not an upstream result or a claim that this work is already implemented.

1. **Establish a functioning visual circuit before training a motor policy.** Our observed all-inhibitory receptor outputs and zero-resting LIF state need an explicit model decision. Test two separately versioned candidates: a calibrated graded visual front end mapped by cell identity into downstream MaleCNS populations, and a declared resting-drive model confined to the relevant lamina populations. Preserve the released anatomical graph and original transmitter metadata. Do not silently reverse signs or manufacture broad firing. Fly-craftax demonstrates that lamina bias can reveal light inhibition while still failing to yield vision-dependent behavior; Closed-Loop Fly demonstrates that a successful front end does not guarantee a useful descending readout. [Craftax calibration and failures](https://github.com/liuzihe02/fly-craftax/blob/main/tracker.md), [rate-to-LIF bridge](https://github.com/ZeroXClem/closed-loop-fly/blob/main/docs/phase3.md).
2. **Prove sensory causality with small, measurable tasks.** Use held-out recordings of left/right motion, looming, darkness and stationary scenes. Record camera exposure/receipt provenance, encoder version, real cell IDs, neural time, wall time and descending features. Compare real, black, frozen and time-shuffled inputs; then disconnect the chosen input or output populations. Require silence or missing evidence to produce zero proposed motion. A rest-subtracted decoder must not turn silence into a steering command. [Closed-Loop Fly corrected interpretation](https://github.com/ZeroXClem/closed-loop-fly/blob/main/docs/followups.md), [Fly64 receipt/action distinction](https://github.com/ornata/fly/blob/main/docs/dashboard-design.md).
3. **Keep a small, identifiable trainable readout.** Once descending features respond appropriately, start with a bounded readout trained offline or in a simulator. Record exactly which parameters change. Compare against a direct sensor-feature controller and equally trained shuffled-graph controls at the same budget. Readout learning is an honest first engineering target; it is not synaptic plasticity inside the fly. Fly Dino provides a particularly clear train/validation/test and readout-silencing example. [Fly Dino protocol](https://github.com/cobanov/flyjump/blob/main/docs/experiment.md).
4. **Define exploration by verified consequences.** A proposed task reward should combine newly observed traversable area with independently checked localization quality, useful image coverage and avoidance of collisions/stalls. Separate raw observations, the derived reward and its eligibility timestamp. Penalize invalid/stale input and unsuccessful actions. Compare against standing still, fixed turning, random safe actions and a simple conventional coverage policy. Darkness must reduce usable-image evidence; a light request must not earn reward until the capability exists and a measured image improves. Craftax shows why an achievement counter alone can reward a repetitive sleep/wake or forward/do routine without visual exploration. [Craftax evaluation](https://github.com/liuzihe02/fly-craftax/blob/main/tracker.md).
5. **Make the LLM a consumer of grounded belief records.** Give it timestamped observations, map uncertainty, actual capabilities, proposed actions and acknowledged outcomes. Let it suggest hypotheses or bounded goals whose effects are subsequently measured. Keep neural inference, LLM advice, reward computation and motor authorization distinct. FLM is an example of a trainable graph-to-language adapter, not evidence that a fly acquires language or an embodied world model. The deployed FlyBrain/Buzz workflow is an example of neural events triggering an external agent workflow, not biological belief formation. [FLM source](https://github.com/nftechie/flm), [FLM preprint](https://artificialscientific.com/papers/flies-are-all-you-need), [FlyBrain deployment](https://flybrain.app/).
6. **Add plasticity only with a falsifiable hypothesis.** If a later experiment updates selected KC-to-MBON edges, retain the original weights, update mask, eligibility state, reward history and checkpoints. Test frozen weights, zero reward, shuffled/yoked reward and delayed credit assignment. Evaluate with learning disabled on unseen scenes and seeds. Neither a dopamine-cell pulse nor changed weights proves improved behavior. [DOOMFLY protocol](https://github.com/nftechie/doomfly/blob/main/docs/doom-live-training.md), [FlyPong negative learning result](https://github.com/jonatasperaza/FlyPong).

All physical-motion demonstrations remain subject to the existing acknowledgement repair and operator-presence gate. This research does not satisfy that gate or close the console-parity or driving tickets.

## Research boundary and reuse

This audit found **real numerical learning** in some external readouts and in Flyhard's engineered graph parameters, and **negative or incomplete behavioral evidence** in several purported plasticity systems. These are different results. A useful controller can arise from a task-specific encoder, strong authored mechanics or a trained external readout even when the measured topology adds no advantage.

No root license found means only that LICENSE, LICENSE.md and license.md were absent at the inspected branch; it is not a legal conclusion about every file. The code/data/artwork distinctions in the table must survive any reuse. The list's CC0 license does not cover linked projects. In particular, Fly Dino's custom attribution terms, Faiku's noncommercial license and the CC BY-NC datasets in several desktop projects cannot be treated as MIT material.

The next defensible milestone is therefore a **measured visual-input → causally responsive descending feature → bounded readout → verified physical outcome** experiment, with silent/black/frozen/disconnected and ordinary-controller controls. Coverage reward, adaptation and LLM-supported planning should build on that evidence, while reporting their own contribution separately.
